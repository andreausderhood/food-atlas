## Overview

Books still hold knowledge no dataset carries: which part of a plant is eaten and how it is prepared, what a cultivar tastes like, and how a food entered a cuisine. For the Food Atlas they serve as the editorial and structural reference shelf, not as bulk imports.

## Species encyclopedias

| Book | Scope | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hanelt (ed.), *Mansfeld's Encyclopedia of Agricultural and Horticultural Crops* (Springer, 2001, 6 vols) | 6,100 cultivated species | The deepest cultivated-species reference, and the source of the infraspecific ranks |
| Wiersema & León, *World Economic Plants*, 2nd ed. (CRC, 2013) | 12,000+ economically important vascular plants | Standard names and uses |
| T.K. Lim, *Edible Medicinal and Non-Medicinal Plants* (Springer, 2012–2016, vols 1–12) | 1,000+ plants | **Closest to the atlas entry format:** taxonomy, names, origin and distribution, agroecology, edible parts, botany, nutritive and medicinal properties |
| Facciola, *Cornucopia II* (1998) | 3,000 species, 7,000 varieties | The widest species-to-variety book; names the edible organ and the preparation |
| van Wyk, *Food Plants of the World*, 2nd ed. (2019) | About 380 plants in depth, 800+ in a checklist | The visual model, with over 1,000 colour photographs |
| Janick & Paull (eds), *The Encyclopedia of Fruit and Nuts* (CABI, 2008) | Temperate and tropical fruit and nut species | Botany, origins, breeding and cultivars |

## Composition in book form

- **Souci, Fachmann & Kraut**, *Food Composition and Nutrition Tables*, 8th ed. (2016): about 800 foods, 260 constituents, with means and ranges.
- **McCance and Widdowson's**, as the CoFID dataset: the UK reference.
- **Simmonds & Preedy (eds)**, *Nutritional Composition of Fruit Cultivars* (Academic Press, 2016): the only book-length source of cultivar-level nutrition.

## Culinary, cultural and scientific

- Davidson & Jaine, *The Oxford Companion to Food*, 3rd ed. (2014): 2,650 A–Z entries, the editorial-voice reference.
- Kiple & Ornelas (eds), *The Cambridge World History of Food* (2000).
- Harold McGee, *On Food and Cooking* (2004): the model for explaining texture, colour and flavour per ingredient.
- Belitz, Grosch & Schieberle, *Food Chemistry*, 4th ed. (2009): molecules per food group, including aroma compounds.
- Page & Dornenburg, *The Flavor Bible* (2008) and Briscione, *The Flavor Matrix* (2018): culinary flavour vocabulary.

## Varieties, fungi, insects and atlas design

- Morgan & Richards, *The New Book of Apples* (2002): about 2,000 apple varieties.
- Robinson, Harding & Vouillamoz, *Wine Grapes* (2012): 1,368 grape varieties with parentage.
- Boa, *Wild Edible Fungi* (FAO, 2004) and van Huis et al., *Edible Insects* (FAO, 2013).
- Johnson & Robinson, *The World Atlas of Wine*, 8th ed. (2019) and Millstone & Lang, *The Atlas of Food*, 2nd ed. (2008): how maps and data can carry a food story.
