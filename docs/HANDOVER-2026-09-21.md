You are continuing the build of the **Food Atlas**: a traceable atlas of edible life.
Every published value carries `value | unit | basis | food_part | taxon | method | n | source DOI | license`.
The non-negotiable frame is the **traceability contract** (`product.md` §3): ten rules whose
essence is — primary evidence only, no amount without a stated basis, no food part by
assumption, bioactivity is never a health claim, every value quotable verbatim, unknown is a
valid answer, licenses are read not guessed.

## 1. Architecture (settled, do not relitigate)

- **Source of truth = the git-versioned file ledger.** `data/registry/*.yaml` (sources,
  foods, organisms, nutrients, molecules, processes), curated claims, `data/build/atlas.json`
  (compiled). Git is the audit ledger; every value is diffable to its source.
- **`atlas.db`** — a deterministically compiled SQLite mirror (planned, not yet built) for
  querying; never the source of truth.
- **Propose-only:** nothing auto-publishes. Ingests produce claims/gaps; curation is human
  (or explicitly gated). Research agents never gain write authority beyond research outputs.
- **License gate (rule 9):** every source needs `license_class` (`ship | cite | adapter |
  separate | unknown`) from the **license text itself**. `unknown` blocks import. No
  venue-reputation guessing.
